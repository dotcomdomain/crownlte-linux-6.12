Crownlte S2DOS05 read-only diagnostic (v23a)
=========================================

The standalone arch/arm64/boot/dts/exynos/exynos9810-crownlte-romram-v23a.dts
is the external panel-bright-work/dtb/v22a test DT verbatim, followed by the
diagnostic bus additions. Build with dtc -I dts -O dtb; no preprocessor needed.
Enable CONFIG_CROWNLTE_S2DOS05_DIAG=y with the integration-3 ROM-in-RAM config.

Reference: /root/crownlte-hid-kernel/ on gekko, drivers/regulator/s2dos05.c,
include/linux/regulator/s2dos05.h and arch/arm64/boot/dts/exynos/
exynos9810-crownlte_eur_open_26.dts. No TOPSYS_STAT/STAT bit names are defined
in that header: these are raw bytes. EN bits 0..4 mean LDO1..4/BUCK1.
IRQ bits 5..0 mean PWRMT/TSD/SSD/SCP/UVLO/OCD.

The bus is dedicated CMGP I2C02, not the adjacent USI_CMGP02 protocol bus:
rate clock CMGP_USI_I2C=95, gate GATE_I2C_CMGP02=105, pins gpm12-0/gpm13-0
function 3, GIC SPI 5 level high. The new wrapper-only compatible
samsung,exynos9810-i2c-wrapper takes reg, pclk/ipclk clocks and optional
samsung,clkreq-on, with no samsung,sysreg/mode. It clears local reset at
+0xc4 and sets clock request at +0xc8 as on the other diagnostic buses;
it does not touch the disabled 4.9 selector at 0x14212024.
The wrapper and I2C child have disjoint MMIO resources.

samsung,crownlte-s2dos05-diag is an isolated I2C client at 0x60 with no IRQ,
regulator children, supplies or real-regulator compatible. The driver has
no PMIC write API. Its one-byte address phase is necessary to select the
register for a repeated-START read, and sends no register data value.
It calls the adapter once under its segment lock, bypassing core retries.
On failure it aborts that dump, logs the errno and waits for the next
scheduled sample; there is no retry of the failed read.

Samples target 1, 5.5, 9, 25, 60, 120 seconds since boot (boottime clock).
Actual uptime is logged; late probe/scheduling can delay samples. The 9s
sample is the requested timed alternative to a panel-recovery completion
hook, not proof that recovery has completed. Each sample reads 0x00..0x10
individually; a dump is not an atomic hardware snapshot. Only the 120s
sample attempts 0x11, exactly once even on error, then stops. IRQ read
clears latched evidence; a failed transfer may already have consumed it.
Bind/unbind sysfs attributes are suppressed and the driver is built-in.

Search dmesg for "s2dos05-diag:". The armed line describes the schedule.
Each sample header reports target_ms, uptime_ms and whether IRQ is read.
Per-register lines and EN decode follow; the final IRQ line names each bit.
No hardware testing was performed: do not infer bus/PMIC availability
from a successful build. The kernel I2C lock cannot serialize any secure
firmware/bootloader coprocessor access. Controller clock/reset/pinmux
changes may affect such an owner. Reading power-meter data may have
device-specific side effects; IRQ definitely clears on read. No write
changes to PMIC protection, voltage, enable, IRQ mask or ADC configuration.
